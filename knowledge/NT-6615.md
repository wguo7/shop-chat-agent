# NextLED Spot Flood and UV Pen Light (NT-6615)

## What it is
The NextLED NT-6615 is a rechargeable 3 in 1 pen light with a focused spot, a wider flood, and a UV light. It is pocket sized with a clip and a magnet on the end for hands free use, and the UV mode helps spot stains and leaks. It is a general purpose mechanic pen light, so it does not carry a CRI color matching rating like the detailing pen light.

## Key features
- 3 lights in 1, a spot, a flood, and a UV light
- Spot for highly focused bright light
- UV light to help detect stains and leaks
- English and metric scales on the pen body
- Magnetic pocket clip and a strong magnet on the end of the pen
- Soft end caps that will not scratch surfaces
- Durable and impact resistant, rechargeable

## Common questions
**How long does the battery last on the NT-6615?**
The spot runs 3 hrs, the flood runs 2 hrs on high and 8 hrs on low, on a full charge.

**How bright is the NT-6615?**
The spot is 200 lm, and the flood is 300 lm on high and 100 lm on low. It also has a UV light at 365 nm.

**Is the NT-6615 water or dust resistant?**
No water or dust rating is published for it. It is rated IK07 for impact resistance.

**How do I charge the NT-6615 and how long does it take?**
It is rechargeable and a full charge takes about 2 hrs.

**What comes in the box with the NT-6615?**
The pen light with its included lithium-ion battery.

**What is the NT-6615 best used for?**
It is best as a pocket 3 in 1 light for mechanics, with a spot for tight low light spots, a flood for general work, and a UV mode to find stains and leaks. It is not a color matching tool, so use the detailing pen light for paint color work.

**Does the NT-6615 have a UV light?**
Yes. It has a UV light at 365 nm to help detect stains and leaks.

## How this differs from similar NextLED lights
The nearest sibling is the NT-6615K detailing pen light. The NT-6615 has no CRI rating and states no color temperature, and it runs a 200 lm spot with a 300 lm and 100 lm flood. The NT-6615K is rated CRI 95 plus at 6000 K for color matching and detailing, and it is brighter with a 280 lm spot and a 430 lm high flood. The NT-6615 sits in the Mechanic Work Lights line, while the NT-6615K sits in the Auto Paint and Detail Lights line.

## Warranty
1 year limited warranty.

## Safety notes
- LEDs are extremely bright. Do not shine the light directly into eyes, as it may cause injury.
- This light has a UV mode. Avoid looking into the UV light and avoid skin exposure.
