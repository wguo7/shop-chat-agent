# NextLED Folding Rechargeable Slim Bar LED Work Light (NT-W2301)

## What it is
The NextLED NT-W2301 is a slim, rechargeable LED work light built to slide into tight places like engine bays and narrow gaps. It gives you a wide flood for filling an area with light plus a focused spot on the end of the bar. A magnetic base and a swiveling, rotating bar let you set it down and work hands free. It is a compact task light, so it is not meant to replace a large tripod work light for lighting a whole bay.

## Key features
- Slim, compact design that reaches into tight places
- Maximum 600 lm output
- 3 light sources, a 500 lm and a 155 lm flood plus a 65 lm spot on the end of the bar
- Comfort grip for non slip use
- Universal swivel and rotating light bar to aim the light where you want it
- Magnetic base for hands free use
- Power status indicator
- Recharges with the included USB-C cable

## Common questions
**How long does the battery last on the NT-W2301?**
The flood runs 2.5 to 7.5 hrs depending on the brightness level, and the spot on the end runs up to 20 hrs on a full charge.

**How bright is the NT-W2301?**
The flood gives a 500 lm high level and a 155 lm lower level, and the spot adds 65 lm, for a maximum output of 600 lm.

**Is the NT-W2301 water resistant?**
Yes. It is rated IPX4, which protects it against splashing water from any direction.

**How do I charge the NT-W2301 and how long does it take?**
Charge it with the included USB-C cable from a 5V 2A supply. A full charge takes about 4 hrs.

**What comes in the box with the NT-W2301?**
The slim bar work light with its built in 3.7V 2200 mAh lithium-ion battery and a USB-C charging cable.

**What is the NT-W2301 best used for?**
It is best for reaching into tight engine bays and work spaces where you want a slim, magnetic, hands free flood with a spot on the end. It is not the right tool for lighting a whole shop, where a tripod work light fits better.

**How far does the NT-W2301 beam reach?**
The flood reaches 50 to 95 ft and the spot reaches 30 ft.

1 year limited warranty.
