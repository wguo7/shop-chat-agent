# NextLED Rechargeable 3-in-1 Quick Connect Light Kit (NT-LS002373)

## What it is
The NextLED NT-LS002373 is a rechargeable 3 in 1 Quick Connect Light Kit with three light heads that snap onto a common base. It includes a slim bar light, a flashlight, and a flex shaft light, plus a USB charging cord. The heads are interchangeable, so you swap the one that fits the job. It is a versatile kit for general tasks rather than a single purpose light.

## Key features
- 3 in 1 Quick Connect kit with three interchangeable light heads
- A 400 and 160 lumen foldable slim bar light
- A 320 and 140 lumen flashlight
- A 120 and 70 lumen flex shaft light
- A USB charging cord
- Replaceable 18650 lithium-ion battery, indoor and outdoor use

## Common questions
**How long does the battery last on the NT-LS002373?**
It depends on the head. The slim bar light runs 4 hrs high and 8 hrs low, the flashlight runs 4 hrs high and 10 hrs low, and the flex shaft light runs 10 hrs high and 16 hrs low.

**How bright is the NT-LS002373?**
The slim bar light is 400 lm high and 160 lm low, the flashlight is 320 lm high and 140 lm low, and the flex shaft light is 120 lm high and 70 lm low.

**Is the NT-LS002373 water resistant?**
Yes. It is rated IPX4 against splashing water.

**How do I charge the NT-LS002373 and how long does it take?**
Charge it over USB with the included cord, which works with any regular 5V USB charger. A full charge takes approximately 6 hrs based on a 1A supply, and the 18650 battery is replaceable. Note that the 4 hr and 8 hr figures are the slim bar light runtimes per charge, not the charging time.

**What comes in the box with the NT-LS002373?**
The slim bar light, the flashlight, the flex shaft light, the common base, and a USB charging cord. All three heads are interchangeable.

**What is the NT-LS002373 best used for?**
It is best when you want one rechargeable kit that covers several jobs, swapping between a slim bar area light, a flashlight, and a flex shaft light on a common base. It is not a single purpose work light.

## Warranty
1 year limited warranty.

## Safety notes
- LEDs are extremely bright. Do not shine the light directly into eyes, as it may cause injury.
- Follow the lithium battery safety guidance in the manual. Do not drop, open, short circuit, puncture, or incinerate the battery, and do not expose it to extreme temperatures.
