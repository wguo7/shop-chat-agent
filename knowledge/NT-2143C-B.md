# NextLED Professional Rechargeable Motion-Sensing Detailing Headlamp (NT-2143C-B)

## What it is
The NextLED NT-2143C-B is a hands free detailing headlamp with a motion sensor, so you wave your hand to turn the light on and off. It is rated CRI 95 plus and has three color temperatures for color matching and finding imperfections. It is a head worn detailing and color match light, not an area work light.

## Key features
- Motion sensor, wave your hand in front of the sensor to turn the light on and off
- CRI 95 plus rated for professional color accuracy
- 3 color temperatures for critical color matching
- Non slip strips on the headband to keep the light secure
- Hands free head worn design

Charge it over USB-C. A full charge takes about 3 hrs. The indicator is red while charging and green when fully charged.

**How does the wave sensor on the NT-2143C-B work?**
Wave your hand in front of the sensor to turn the light on, and wave again to turn it off, so you can keep your hands on the work.

## Troubleshooting
**The light will not charge.**
This light can fail to charge if it is connected to an adapter that is too powerful. Do not use an adapter rated over 3 amps or 8 volts, because that can trip the battery's overcharge protection and prevent it from charging. Use a standard 5V USB charger with the supplied cable.
