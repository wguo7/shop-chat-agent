# NextLED 10" Triple Light Mode Under Car LED Work Light (NT-6648)

## What it is
The NextLED NT-6648 is a low profile rolling work light made to slide under a car on four steel wheels. It has three modes, a bright disc flood, an 18 in flexible detail light with a zoom focus, and a UVA light for leak detection. It runs on its batteries or plugged in. It is built for under car and floor level work, not for mounting up high.

## Key features
- Three light modes, a flood disc light, an 18 in flexible detail light, and a UVA light
- 18 in flexible detail light with a zoom focus
- 4 stainless steel roller wheels for easy sliding on concrete
- Low profile to fit under a car
- Dual power source, corded and rechargeable
- UVA light for leak detection

## Common questions
**How long does the battery last on the NT-6648?**
The disc light runs 6 hrs and the flexible detail light runs 10 hrs on a full charge.

**How bright is the NT-6648?**
The disc light is 500 lm and the flexible detail light is 200 lm. It also has a UVA light for leak detection.

**Is the NT-6648 water or dust resistant?**
Yes. It is rated IP-43 against water and dust.

**How do I charge the NT-6648 and how long does it take?**
Charge it with the included micro USB cable. A full charge takes about 3 hrs. The 5V 2A charger is not included, and the light can also run while plugged in.

**What comes in the box with the NT-6648?**
The under car work light with two 3.7V 5200 mAh lithium-ion batteries and the charging cable. The wall charger is not included.

**What is the NT-6648 best used for?**
It is best for under car and floor level work, rolled on its steel wheels, with the disc flood for wide light, the 18 in flexible arm for detail, and the UVA mode for finding leaks. It is not made for mounting up high.

**Does the NT-6648 have a UV light?**
Yes. The disc light has a UVA switch for leak detection.

## Warranty
1 year limited warranty.

## Safety notes
- LEDs are extremely bright. Do not shine the light directly into eyes, as it may cause injury.
- This light has a UVA mode. Avoid looking into the UV light and avoid skin exposure.
- Follow the lithium battery safety guidance in the manual. Do not drop, open, short circuit, puncture, or incinerate the batteries, and do not expose them to extreme temperatures.
