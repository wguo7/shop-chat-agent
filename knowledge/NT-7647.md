# NextLED Flexible Shaft LED Work Light (NT-7647)

## What it is
The NextLED NT-7647 is a battery powered work light on a flexible shaft, built to bend into tight spots and stay where you put it. It uses a Cree XPG LED with a zoom head so you can go from wide flood to tighter beam, and a magnetic base holds it hands free. It runs on AA batteries, so it is a grab and go work light rather than a rechargeable one.

## Key features
- Flexible neck that holds its position
- Zoom in and out lamp head design
- Cree XPG high performance LED
- Top mounted LED for extra light
- Magnetic base for hands free use
- Heavy duty aluminum housing
- Battery operated, runs on 3 AA batteries

## Common questions
**How long does the NT-7647 run on a set of batteries?**
It runs 8 hrs on high and 20 hrs on low on a set of AA batteries.

**How bright is the NT-7647?**
The main LED gives 200 lm on high and 80 lm on low, and there is a 45 lm top mounted LED.

**Is the NT-7647 water resistant?**
Yes. It is rated IP-54 against water and dust.

**Does the NT-7647 recharge, and how is it powered?**
It does not recharge. It runs on 3 AA batteries, which are not included.

**What comes in the box with the NT-7647?**
The flexible shaft work light. The 3 AA batteries are not included.

**What is the NT-7647 best used for?**
It is best for hands free lighting in tight spots where you bend the shaft into place and stick the magnet to metal, with the zoom head set wide or narrow. It is not a rechargeable light, so keep spare AA batteries on hand.

**How far does the NT-7647 beam reach?**
The beam reaches 325 ft.

## How this differs from similar NextLED lights
The NT-7647 is the base flexible shaft work light in this family. The NT-7647-1 uses the same body but adds a magnetic pick up tool head that lifts up to 1 lb and extends to 24 in, and it is brighter at 300 lm high against 200 lm high here. The NT-7647-F is the grill light version of this body with a stated warm 3200 K beam, where the NT-7647 states no color temperature and adds a 45 lm top LED. The NT-7728 is a separate grill light that uses a CREE LED at 210 lm high and 60 lm low with an IPX4 rating and a 1.21 lb weight, against this light's Cree XPG LED at 200 lm high and 80 lm low with an IP-54 rating. All four run on 3 AA batteries.

## Warranty
1 year limited warranty.

## Safety notes
- LEDs are extremely bright. Do not shine the light directly into eyes, as it may cause injury.
