# NextLED Double Sided LED Task Light (NT-7544)

## What it is
The NextLED NT-7544 is a folding, rechargeable task light with light bars on both sides for putting bright even light over a work area. You can run one side, both sides, or just a small tip light, and it folds and swivels to aim where you need it. A magnetic base holds it hands free, and it can run on its battery or while plugged in. It is a general task light, not a focused spotlight for long range beams.

## Key features
- Foldable, swivel design
- Dual light bar function with single, both, tip, and off modes
- Magnetic base for hands free use
- Dual power source, corded and rechargeable
- Red and green charging indicator
- Aluminum construction
- Recharges with the included USB-C cable

## Common questions
**How long does the battery last on the NT-7544?**
It runs 6 hrs with one side on, 3.5 hrs with both sides on, and 24 hrs on the tip light, on a full charge.

**How bright is the NT-7544?**
One side gives 600 lm, both sides give 1200 lm, and the tip light gives 50 lm.

**Is the NT-7544 water resistant?**
Yes. It is rated IPX4, which protects it against splashing water.

**How do I charge the NT-7544 and how long does it take?**
Charge it with the included USB-C cable. A full charge takes about 4 hrs. A 5V 1A adaptor is not included, so use a standard USB power source. It can also run while plugged in.

**What comes in the box with the NT-7544?**
The work light with its included 18650 3.7V 2900 mAh battery and a USB charging cable. A wall adaptor is not included.

**What is the NT-7544 best used for?**
It is best for lighting a work area from both sides on a bench or under the hood, folded and aimed where you need it, on battery or corded. It is not the right tool when you need a focused long range spot beam.

## How this differs from similar NextLED lights
The nearest sibling is the NT-7543 Folding Bar LED Task Light. The NT-7543 is a single bar light at 350 lm high, while the NT-7544 is the double sided model with two bars that reach 1200 lm with both sides on, plus a 50 lm tip light. The NT-7544 also uses a larger 2900 mAh battery, compared with 2200 mAh on the NT-7543.

1 year limited warranty.
