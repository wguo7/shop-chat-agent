# NextLED Dual Power Tripod LED Work Light (NT-6926)

## What it is
The NextLED NT-6926 is a portable tripod work light for lighting a whole work area such as a bay or a corner of a shop. It runs cordless on its batteries or plugged into an outlet, and the lamp head lifts off the tripod with a magnetic base so you can place it where you need it. It has white and warm modes and three brightness levels. It is a larger area light, not a pocket or handheld tool.

## Key features
- Multi use tripod work light that configures for many jobs
- Two illumination settings, white and warm
- Cordless with batteries included, and also works while plugged in
- Heavy duty tripod that extends and folds
- Removable head with a magnetic base for use on or off the tripod
- 360 degree rotatable top bar

## Common questions
**How long does the battery last on the NT-6926?**
On a full charge it runs 1 to 2 hrs on high, 5 to 6 hrs on medium, and 12 to 14 hrs on low.

**How bright is the NT-6926?**
It has three battery levels at 5000 lm high, 1500 lm medium, and 500 lm low, and it puts out 1800 lm when running on the cord.

**Is the NT-6926 water or dust resistant?**
No water or dust rating is published for it.

**How do I charge the NT-6926 and how long does it take?**
Charge it with the included 8.4V 3A charger and cable. A full charge takes about 4 hrs. It can also run while plugged into an outlet.

**What comes in the box with the NT-6926?**
The tripod work light with its removable head, two 7.4V 4000 mAh lithium-ion batteries, and the charger with cable.

**What is the NT-6926 best used for?**
It is best for lighting a whole work area from the tripod, or lifting the magnetic head off the tripod to place it on metal nearby. It is not a pocket or handheld light.

**What color settings does the NT-6926 have?**
It has a warm setting at 4000 K and a bright setting at 6500 K.

## How this differs from similar NextLED lights
The nearest sibling is the NT-6926M ULTIMATE tripod. The NT-6926 is the standard model with a 1 year warranty, no remote, a 5000 lm high level, and two 7.4V 4000 mAh batteries. The NT-6926M is the ULTIMATE model with a 3 year warranty, a wireless remote control, a higher 6000 lm high level and 2000 lm medium level, and four 7.4V 8000 mAh batteries for longer runtime. The NT-6926 sits in the Mechanic Work Lights line, while the NT-6926M sits in the Auto Paint and Detail Lights line.

## Warranty
1 year limited warranty.

## Safety notes
- LEDs are extremely bright. Do not shine the light directly into eyes, as it may cause injury.
- Follow the lithium battery safety guidance in the manual. Do not drop, open, short circuit, puncture, or incinerate the batteries, and do not expose them to extreme temperatures.
