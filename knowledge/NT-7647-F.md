# NextLED Flexible Shaft LED Grill Light (NT-7647-F)

## What it is
The NextLED NT-7647-F is a battery powered grill light on a flexible shaft, made to clamp onto a grill with its magnetic base and bend the warm beam over the cooking surface. It uses a warm 3200 K light and a wide angle head. It is a grill and outdoor cooking light, not a shop work light.

## Key features
- 200 lumen battery powered LED grill light
- Slim, compact design
- Strong magnetic base that sticks to metal grill surfaces
- Flexible neck with a wide angle head
- Warm, natural 3200 K light
- Intended for indoor and outdoor use

## Common questions
**How long does the NT-7647-F run on a set of batteries?**
It runs 8 hrs on high and 20 hrs on low on a set of AA batteries.

**How bright is the NT-7647-F?**
It gives 200 lm on high and 80 lm on low, with a warm 3200 K light.

**Is the NT-7647-F water resistant?**
Yes. It is rated IP-54 against water and dust.

**Does the NT-7647-F recharge, and how is it powered?**
It does not recharge. It runs on 3 AA batteries, which are not included.

**What comes in the box with the NT-7647-F?**
The flexible shaft grill light. The 3 AA batteries are not included.

**What is the NT-7647-F best used for?**
It is best for lighting a grill, clamped on with its magnetic base and bent over the cooking surface with the warm beam. It is not a shop work light.

## How this differs from similar NextLED lights
The NT-7647-F is a grill light version of the 7647 flexible shaft body, with a warm 3200 K beam at 200 lm high and 80 lm low and a 0.58 lb weight. The NT-7728 is the other grill light and is heavier at 1.21 lb, with a CREE LED at 210 lm high and 60 lm low, an IPX4 rating, and infinite brightness adjustability, against this light's IP-54 rating and two fixed levels. The NT-7647 is the work light version of the body, which uses a Cree XPG LED and adds a 45 lm top mounted LED but states no color temperature. All three run on 3 AA batteries.
