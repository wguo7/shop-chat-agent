# NextLED Folding Bar LED Task Light (NT-7543)

## What it is
The NextLED NT-7543 is a folding, rechargeable single bar task light for putting even light over a work area. It has a high and a low level, a pivoting bar to aim the light, and a comfort grip for non slip use. A magnetic base holds it hands free, and it can run on its battery or while plugged in. It is a general task light, not a focused spotlight for long range beams.

## Key features
- Foldable design
- Pivoting light bar to direct light where you need it
- Magnetic base for hands free use
- Dual power source, corded and rechargeable
- Comfort grip for non slip operation
- Red and green charging indicator
- Replaceable 18650 lithium-ion battery
- Recharges with the included USB-C cable

## Common questions
**How long does the battery last on the NT-7543?**
It runs 2 hrs on the high level and 24 hrs on the low level on a full charge.

**How bright is the NT-7543?**
It has two levels, 350 lm on high and 50 lm on low.

**Is the NT-7543 water resistant?**
Yes. It is rated IPX4, which protects it against splashing water.

**How do I charge the NT-7543 and how long does it take?**
Charge it with the included USB-C cable. A full charge takes about 4 hrs. A 5V 1A adaptor is not included, so use a standard USB power source. It can also run while plugged in.

**What comes in the box with the NT-7543?**
The work light with its included and replaceable 18650 3.7V 2200 mAh battery and a USB-C charging cable. A wall adaptor is not included.

**What is the NT-7543 best used for?**
It is best for lighting a work area with a single folding bar on a bench or under the hood, aimed with the pivoting bar, on battery or corded. It is not the right tool when you need a focused long range spot beam.

## How this differs from similar NextLED lights
The nearest sibling is the NT-7544 Double Sided LED Task Light. The NT-7543 is the single bar model at 350 lm high, while the NT-7544 adds a second bar that reaches 1200 lm with both sides on plus a 50 lm tip light. The NT-7543 uses a 2200 mAh battery, compared with 2900 mAh on the NT-7544.
