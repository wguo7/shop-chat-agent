# NextLED Flexible Shaft LED Task Light with Magnetic Pick-Up Tool (NT-7647-1)

## What it is
The NextLED NT-7647-1 is a battery powered task light on a flexible shaft with a magnetic head that doubles as a pick up tool for dropped metal parts. It uses a Cree XPG LED with a zoom head, bends into tight spots, and holds its position. It runs on AA batteries, so it is a grab and go tool rather than a rechargeable one.

## Key features
- Improved LED head with magnetic tool pick up function
- Flexible neck that holds its position
- Zoom in and out lamp head design
- Cree XPG high performance LED
- Magnetic base for hands free use
- High and low light function
- Battery operated, runs on 3 AA batteries

## Common questions
**How long does the NT-7647-1 run on a set of batteries?**
It runs 8 hrs on high and 20 hrs on low on a set of AA batteries.

**How bright is the NT-7647-1?**
It gives 300 lm on high and 80 lm on low.

**Is the NT-7647-1 water resistant?**
Yes. It is rated IP-54 against water and dust.

**Does the NT-7647-1 recharge, and how is it powered?**
It does not recharge. It runs on 3 AA batteries, which are not included.

**What comes in the box with the NT-7647-1?**
The flexible shaft task light with its magnetic pick up head. The 3 AA batteries are not included.

**What is the NT-7647-1 best used for?**
It is best for working in tight spots while also retrieving dropped metal parts, since the magnetic head picks up to 1 lb and the shaft extends to 24 in. It is not a rechargeable light, so keep spare AA batteries on hand.

**How much can the magnetic pick up tool lift?**
The magnetic head picks up a maximum of 1 lb, and the shaft extends to 24 in to reach into tight spaces.

## How this differs from similar NextLED lights
The NT-7647-1 adds the magnetic pick up tool head, which lifts up to 1 lb and extends to 24 in, and it runs at 300 lm high. The base NT-7647 has no pick up tool, runs at 200 lm high, and adds a 45 lm top mounted LED. The NT-7647-F is the grill light version of the body at 200 lm high with a stated warm 3200 K beam and no pick up tool. The NT-7728 is a separate grill light using a CREE LED at 210 lm high and 60 lm low with an IPX4 rating. All four run on 3 AA batteries.
