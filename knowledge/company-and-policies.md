# NextLED Company and Policies

## About NextLED
NextLED is a brand of Nextool Inc. that offers battery operated and rechargeable LED lighting in three areas, Automotive, Home, and Construction Job Site. The company has provided value and innovation for distributors and customers since 2009. Its global headquarters in Schaumburg, Illinois includes Marketing, Product Management, Engineering, Research and Development, and Customer Support. The team focuses on understanding users and innovating on new and existing LED solutions, and integrity, quality, and workmanship go into every product.

## Warranty terms
NextLED provides a 1 year warranty on most products. The ULTIMATE series carries a 3 year warranty. The ULTIMATE series is exactly three models, the NT-6692M, the NT-7885M, and the NT-6926M. Every other NextLED product carries the standard 1 year warranty. This limited warranty covers defects in material or damage on arrival.

## Authorized retailers versus third party sellers
The limited warranty covers only customers who purchase NextLED products directly from mynextled.com or from authorized retailers or resellers. If you purchased from a third party seller, please contact that seller directly, because NextLED cannot provide a refund for those purchases. The limited warranty does not apply to products bought from unauthorized resellers, and NextLED is not responsible for refund or replacement of those products. To make a warranty claim you must have your original receipt of purchase.

## Returns and refunds
NextLED accepts returns for refund when the request is made within 14 days of purchase, with the original receipt, and when the product was purchased directly from mynextled.com. A customer may receive a replacement beyond 14 days if the issue is a warranty or defective product matter. A customer who returns a product within the window due to buyer's remorse is responsible for all return shipping costs. A customer who receives a defective item gets a return shipping label and is not responsible for return shipping.

## 14 day return window
Returns for refund must be requested within 14 days of purchase with the original receipt. No returns for refund are accepted after 14 days. Beyond 14 days, a replacement may still be available for a warranty or defective product issue.

## Shipping
Orders usually process in less than 24 hours. Orders placed with the free shipping option should arrive within 3 to 5 business days of the day the order was placed. For orders over 15 pounds or shipping outside the contiguous 48 states, shipping is calculated by the delivery partners, which are USPS, UPS, and FedEx, based on the order weight and the shipping location.

## Free shipping threshold
Free shipping is applied automatically to any order with a total of 40 dollars or greater, as long as the total order weight does not exceed 15 pounds and the shipping address is within the contiguous United States, which is the 48 physically connected states.

## Charging troubleshooting for NT-2143C-B and NT-5571
This guidance applies to the NT-2143C-B motion sensing detailing headlamp and the NT-5571 drop light. If either light will not charge, use this guidance.

This light can fail to charge if it is connected to an adapter that is too powerful. Do not use an adapter rated over 3 amps or 8 volts, because that can trip the battery's overcharge protection and prevent it from charging. Use a standard 5V USB charger with the supplied cable.
