# NextLED Rechargeable Dimmable Neck Light (NT-6633)

## What it is
The NextLED NT-6633 is a hands free wrap around neck light with a light on each side, each on its own switch, and full dimming so you can set the exact level you want. It has a flexible band, a strong magnetic bottom, and a battery case in the back with magnets and a tripod mount. It is a wearable task light, not an area work light.

## Key features
- Wrap around design with an adjustable snake light shape
- Dual switch control, a separate switch on each light
- Fully dimmable white LED
- Battery case in the back with magnets and tripod mounting
- Strong magnetic bottom
- Rechargeable over USB-C with the cable included

## Common questions
**How long does the battery last on the NT-6633?**
It runs 3 to 6 hrs at full brightness in single or dual mode, and up to 12 hrs at the lowest setting.

**How bright is the NT-6633?**
It is fully dimmable from 15 to 300 lm.

**Is the NT-6633 water resistant?**
Yes. It is rated IP-X4 against water, and it is impact resistant.

**How do I charge the NT-6633 and how long does it take?**
Charge it over USB-C with the included cable. A full charge takes about 4 hrs. The 5V 1A adaptor is not included. The indicator is red while charging and green when fully charged.

**What comes in the box with the NT-6633?**
The neck light with its included 3.7V 2000 mAh lithium-ion battery and a USB charging cable.

**What is the NT-6633 best used for?**
It is best as a hands free wrap around light for detail work, with a separate switch on each side and full dimming to set the level. It is not an area work light.

**Can I control each light on the NT-6633 separately?**
Yes. It has a dual switch control with a separate switch on each light, so you can run one side or both.

## How this differs from similar NextLED lights
The nearest sibling is the NT-6763 neck light. The NT-6633 is the dimmable wrap around model with a separate switch on each light and fully dimmable output from 15 to 300 lm, plus tripod mounting. The NT-6763 is the zoomable model with an adjustable focus, brightness from 100 to 500 lm, a 215 ft beam, and a replaceable battery. Choose the NT-6633 for independent dual light control with full dimming, and the NT-6763 for adjustable focus and a swappable battery.

## Warranty
1 year limited warranty.

## Safety notes
- LEDs are extremely bright. Do not shine the light directly into eyes, as it may cause injury.
