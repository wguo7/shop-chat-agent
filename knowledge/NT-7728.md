# NextLED LED Grill Light (NT-7728)

## What it is
The NextLED NT-7728 is a battery powered grill light with a CREE LED and a warm 3200 K beam, built to mount on a grill with its ultra strong magnetic base. It has two brightness levels plus infinite adjustability and a lens that spreads a wide, even light. It is a grill and outdoor cooking light, not a shop work light.

## Key features
- Warm, natural 3200 K lighting
- High performance CREE LED
- Two standard brightness levels plus infinite brightness adjustability
- Unique lens design for wider, more even light
- Ultra strong magnetic base
- Powered by 3 AA batteries

## Common questions
**How long does the NT-7728 run on a set of batteries?**
It runs 6 hrs on high and 10 hrs on low on a set of AA batteries.

**How bright is the NT-7728?**
It gives 210 lm on high and 60 lm on low, and the brightness is infinitely adjustable by holding the on and off button. The light is a warm 3200 K.

**Is the NT-7728 water resistant?**
Yes. It is rated IPX4 against splashing water.

**Does the NT-7728 recharge, and how is it powered?**
It does not recharge. It runs on 3 AA batteries, which are not included.

**What comes in the box with the NT-7728?**
The grill light. The 3 AA batteries are not included.

**What is the NT-7728 best used for?**
It is best for lighting a grill, held on with its ultra strong magnetic base, with a warm wide beam over the cooking surface. It is not a shop work light.

## How this differs from similar NextLED lights
The NT-7728 is a grill light with a CREE LED at 210 lm high and 60 lm low, an IPX4 rating, infinite brightness adjustability, and a 1.21 lb weight. The NT-7647-F is the other grill light and is lighter at 0.58 lb, at 200 lm high and 80 lm low with an IP-54 rating and two fixed levels, on the 7647 flexible shaft body. The NT-7647 is the work light version of that body, which uses a Cree XPG LED and adds a 45 lm top mounted LED. All three share a warm or neutral beam and run on 3 AA batteries, and the NT-7647-F and NT-7647 state a 325 ft beam distance.

## Warranty
1 year limited warranty.

## Safety notes
- LEDs are extremely bright. Do not shine the light directly into eyes, as it may cause injury.
