# NextLED Professional Rechargeable Color Matching Light (NT-6692M)

## What it is
The NextLED NT-6692M is an ULTIMATE color matching light for paint and detail work, with five selectable color temperatures and a memory function so it returns to your last setting. It is rated CRI 96 plus for accurate color and has a detachable diffuser to cut glare and reflections. It also has a 200 lm spot for extra light. It is a precise color matching tool, not a general area work light.

## Key features
- 5 color temperatures with a memory function for critical color matching
- CRI 96 plus rated for color accuracy
- Detachable diffuser that reduces glare and reflections
- Dual light mode, a flood lens and a spot
- Multi mount charging base, with a screw mount or a magnetic base
- Rechargeable with the included USB-C cable

## Common questions
**How long does the battery last on the NT-6692M?**
The flood runs up to 4 hrs and the spot runs up to 10 hrs on a full charge.

**How bright is the NT-6692M?**
The flood reaches a maximum of 500 lm and the spot reaches a maximum of 200 lm.

**What color temperatures and CRI does the NT-6692M have?**
It is rated CRI 96 plus and offers five color temperatures at 2700 K, 3500 K, 4500 K, 5500 K, and 6500 K, with a memory function that keeps your last setting.

**Is the NT-6692M water resistant?**
Yes. It is rated IPX6 against powerful water jets.

**How do I charge the NT-6692M and how long does it take?**
Charge it with the included USB-C cable using a 5V 2A supply, or set it on its charging base. A full charge takes about 4.5 hrs. While charging the indicator flashes red, and it turns steady green when fully charged.

**What comes in the box with the NT-6692M?**
The color matching light with its built in 3.7V 4000 mAh battery, the USB-C cable, the charging base, and the detachable diffuser.

**What is the NT-6692M best used for?**
It is best for fine paint color matching and spotting defects such as swirl marks and sanding marks, using its five color temperatures and the diffuser to read color truly. It is not a general area work light.

## How this differs from similar NextLED lights
The nearest sibling is the NT-7885M, the other ULTIMATE color match light. The NT-6692M is rated CRI 96 plus and gives five color temperatures from 2700 K to 6500 K with a memory function, at 500 lm flood and 200 lm spot, with an IPX6 rating. The NT-7885M is rated CRI 95 plus with two color temperatures at 6500 K and 3000 K but is much brighter, up to 1500 lm in Turbo, and adds an SMD spot, a UV light, and a magnetic pick up tip, with IP66 and IK07 ratings. Choose the NT-6692M for more color temperature steps for fine matching, and the NT-7885M for maximum brightness and inspection features. Both are ULTIMATE and carry a 3 year warranty.
