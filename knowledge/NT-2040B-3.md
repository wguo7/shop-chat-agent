# NextLED Professional 3 Temperature Color Matching Light (NT-2040B-3)

## What it is
The NextLED NT-2040B-3 is a handheld color matching light with three color temperatures, rated CRI 96 plus so colors read true for paint work. It has a focus lens, a rotating base with a magnet, and a hidden hook and magnet at the back for hands free use. It charges on a base that can be wall mounted. It is a color matching tool, not a general area work light. Some listings show the number as NT-20408-3, which is the same light.

## Key features
- 3 color temperatures for critical color matching and finding imperfections
- CRI 96 plus rated for color accuracy
- Strong ABS housing with a focus lens
- Rotating base with a magnet that also adjusts the lighting angle
- Hidden hook and magnet at the back for hands free working
- Mountable charging base for a wall mounted charging station

## Common questions
**How long does the battery last on the NT-2040B-3?**
It runs about 2.5 hrs on a full charge.

**How bright is the NT-2040B-3?**
It puts out 500 lm from a 5W COB LED.

**What color temperatures and CRI does the NT-2040B-3 have?**
It is rated CRI 96 plus and has three color temperatures at 6500 K, 4000 K, and 2700 K, selected in sequence with the switch.

**Is the NT-2040B-3 water and dust resistant?**
Yes. It is rated IP-66 against water and dust.

**How do I charge the NT-2040B-3 and how long does it take?**
Charge it with the Micro USB cable on its charging base, which can be wall mounted. A full charge takes about 4 hrs.

**What comes in the box with the NT-2040B-3?**
The color matching light with its built in 3.7V 2200 mAh battery, the Micro USB cable, and the charging base.

**What is the NT-2040B-3 best used for?**
It is best for paint color matching and spotting defects such as swirl marks and sanding marks, using its three color temperatures. It is not a general area work light.

**I have a unit or listing marked NT-20408-3. Is that the same light?**
Yes. NT-20408-3 is a transposed form of the same model, the NT-2040B-3.
