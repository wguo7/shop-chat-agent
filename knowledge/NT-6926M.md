# NextLED Remote Control Dual Power Tripod LED Work Light (NT-6926M)

## What it is
The NextLED NT-6926M is the ULTIMATE remote control tripod work light for lighting a whole area such as a bay, a shop, or a job site. It runs cordless on its batteries or plugged into an outlet, and a wireless remote lets you adjust the light without stopping work. The lamp head lifts off the tripod on a magnetic base. It is a large area light with extended run time, not a handheld tool. The manual prints the model as NT-6926-M.

## Key features
- Wireless remote control to adjust lighting without interrupting work
- CRI 90 plus rated
- Two illumination settings, white and warm
- Three brightness levels plus a corded level
- Flexible power, run cordless on batteries or plugged in
- Removable head with a magnetic base and a 360 degree rotatable top bar
- Large capacity built in batteries for extended run time

## Common questions
**How long does the battery last on the NT-6926M?**
On a full charge it runs 2 to 3 hrs on high, 5 to 6 hrs on medium, and 12 to 14 hrs on low.

**How bright is the NT-6926M?**
It has three battery levels at 6000 lm high, 2000 lm medium, and 500 lm low, and it puts out 1800 lm when running on the cord.

**Is the NT-6926M water or dust resistant?**
No water or dust rating is published for it.

**How do I charge the NT-6926M and how long does it take?**
Charge it with the included 8.4V 3A charger and cable. A full charge takes about 8 hrs. It can also run while plugged into an outlet.

**What comes in the box with the NT-6926M?**
The tripod work light with its removable head, four 7.4V 8000 mAh lithium-ion batteries, the charger with cable, and the wireless remote control.

**What is the NT-6926M best used for?**
It is best for lighting a whole work area from the tripod with remote control, or lifting the magnetic head off the tripod to place it nearby, for auto detailing, painting, drywall, and shop or warehouse work. It is not a handheld light.

**Does the NT-6926M come with a remote?**
Yes. It includes a wireless remote control that adjusts the lighting, and the remote stores magnetically on the light head.

## How this differs from similar NextLED lights
The nearest sibling is the NT-6926, the standard tripod. The NT-6926M is the ULTIMATE model with a 3 year warranty, a wireless remote control, CRI 90 plus, a higher 6000 lm high level and 2000 lm medium level, and four 7.4V 8000 mAh batteries for longer run time. The NT-6926 is the standard model with a 1 year warranty, no remote, no CRI rating, a 5000 lm high level and 1500 lm medium level, and two 7.4V 4000 mAh batteries. The NT-6926M sits in the Auto Paint and Detail Lights line, while the NT-6926 sits in the Mechanic Work Lights line.

## Warranty
3 year limited warranty.

## Safety notes
- LEDs are extremely bright. Do not shine the light directly into eyes, as it may cause injury.
- Follow the lithium battery safety guidance in the manual. Do not drop, open, short circuit, puncture, or incinerate the batteries, and do not expose them to extreme temperatures.
