# NextLED Multi-Function Spot and Wide Beam Headlamp (NT-6598)

## What it is
The NextLED NT-6598 is a rechargeable headlamp with three light sets in one, a wide beam, a tilting spotlight, and a rear red tail light. It has nine functions across three independent switches, a red mode for night vision, and a strap with clips to mount on a hard hat. It is a hands free head worn light for repair, biking, and camping.

## Key features
- Wrap around 3 light design with 9 functions
- Wide beam light with high, low, and red modes
- Tilting spotlight that pivots up to 90 degrees with high, low, and strobe
- Rear red tail light with red, flashing, and sequential modes
- 3 independent switches
- Durable hat strap with 2 clips to mount on a hard hat
- Rechargeable with the included USB charging cable, and a carrying bag

## Common questions
**How long does the battery last on the NT-6598?**
The wide beam runs 2 hrs on high and 6 hrs on low, the spotlight runs 3 hrs on high and 6 hrs on low, and the red tail light runs 24 hrs.

**How bright is the NT-6598?**
The wide beam is 300 lm high and 100 lm low, the spotlight is 200 lm high and 100 lm low with a 200 lm strobe, and the red modes are 20 lm.

**Is the NT-6598 water resistant?**
Yes. It is rated IP54 against water and dust.

**How do I charge the NT-6598 and how long does it take?**
Charge it with the included USB cable. A full charge takes about 2 hrs. The 5V 2A adapter is not provided.

**What comes in the box with the NT-6598?**
The headlamp with its included 3.7V 1500 mAh battery, a USB charging cable, and a carrying bag.

**What is the NT-6598 best used for?**
It is best as a hands free headlamp for repair work, biking, and camping, with a wide beam for general light, a tilting spot for detail, a red mode for night vision, and a rear red safety light. The adapter is not included.

**Does the NT-6598 have a red night vision mode?**
Yes. The wide beam has a red mode, and there is a rear red tail light with steady, flashing, and sequential settings.

## Warranty
1 year limited warranty.

## Safety notes
- LEDs are extremely bright. Do not shine the light directly into eyes, as it may cause injury.
- Charge indoors only.
