# NextLED Rechargeable Handheld Bar LED and UV Work Light (NT-2061A-10UV)

## What it is
The NextLED NT-2061A-10UV is a rechargeable handheld bar work light with a built in UV mode for checking leaks. It has full and half power white light plus a UV setting, a comfort grip, and two hanging hooks for hands free use. It is a general handheld work light, not an area light for a whole bay. The manual prints the factory codes EF-2061A-10UV and 370-5525 for the same product.

## Key features
- Full power, half power, and UV light settings
- 10W COB LED
- Comfort grip for non slip operation
- Two hanging hooks, a top hook and a detachable bottom hook
- UV switch for UVA light to check for leaks
- Durable design, rechargeable with the included USB-C cable

## Common questions
**How long does the battery last on the NT-2061A-10UV?**
It runs 2.5 hrs on high and 5 hrs on low on a full charge.

**How bright is the NT-2061A-10UV?**
It puts out 1000 lm on full power and 500 lm on half power, and it has a UV setting at 395 nm for leak checks.

**Is the NT-2061A-10UV water or dust resistant?**
No water or dust rating is published for it. Its operating temperature range is 23 to 118 °F.

**How do I charge the NT-2061A-10UV and how long does it take?**
Charge it with the included USB-C cable. A full charge takes about 4 hrs based on a 1A supply. The wall charger is not included and is sold separately.

**What comes in the box with the NT-2061A-10UV?**
The handheld bar work light with its built in battery and a USB-C charging cable. The wall charger is not included.

**What is the NT-2061A-10UV best used for?**
It is best as a handheld bar work light for general shop tasks, hung hands free from one of its two hooks, with the UV mode for finding leaks. It is not an area light for a whole bay.

**I have a unit marked EF-2061A-10UV or 370-5525. Is that the same light?**
Yes. EF-2061A-10UV and 370-5525 are factory codes printed on the manual for the same product, the NT-2061A-10UV.

## Warranty
1 year limited warranty.

## Safety notes
- LEDs are extremely bright. Do not shine the light directly into eyes, as it may cause injury.
- This light has a UV mode. Avoid looking into the UV light and avoid skin exposure.
