# NextLED Rechargeable Detachable Under Hood LED and UV Work Light (NT-2220LF-UV)

## What it is
The NextLED NT-2220LF-UV is a rechargeable under hood work light that spans the engine bay on a telescoping bracket and adds a UV mode for finding leaks. The two work lights detach for handheld use, and the bracket folds with a push button. It is an under hood and engine bay area light, not a pocket tool. The body is printed NT-EF2220LF-UV, and some listings show NT-2200LF-UV, both of which are the same light.

## Key features
- High, low, and UV function
- Foldable, adjustable, detachable bracket base for self standing use
- 2 work lights that detach for handheld use
- Soft padded hanging hooks that protect work surfaces
- Battery level indicator
- Push button design with a 10 position stop
- Rechargeable with the USB-C cable included

## Common questions
**How long does the battery last on the NT-2220LF-UV?**
It runs 2 hrs on high and 4 hrs on low on a full charge.

**How bright is the NT-2220LF-UV?**
It puts out 2000 lm on high and 1000 lm on low, and it has a UV mode at 395 nm for leak detection.

**Is the NT-2220LF-UV water resistant?**
No water resistance rating is published for it. Its operating temperature range is 23 to 118 °F.

**How do I charge the NT-2220LF-UV and how long does it take?**
Charge it with the included USB-C cable. A full charge takes about 4 hrs.

**What comes in the box with the NT-2220LF-UV?**
The under hood work light with its two 3.7V 4000 mAh batteries and a USB-C charging cable.

**How wide does the NT-2220LF-UV stretch across the engine bay?**
The telescoping bracket extends from 48 to 77 in to span the engine bay.

**What is the NT-2220LF-UV best used for?**
It is best for lighting an engine bay from above on its telescoping bracket, with a UV mode for finding leaks, and the two lights detach for handheld work. It is not a pocket tool.

## Safety notes
- LEDs are extremely bright. Do not shine the light directly into eyes, as it may cause injury.
- This light has a UV mode. Avoid looking into the UV light and avoid skin exposure.
