# NextLED Cordless Rechargeable Drop Light (NT-5571)

## What it is
The NextLED NT-5571 is a cordless, rechargeable drop light that replaces a traditional corded drop light. It uses a COB LED for a broad even beam and has three brightness levels for different jobs. Two hanging hooks and a strong magnetic base let you position it for hands free work, and a built in power bank can charge other devices. It is built for indoor use, so it is not made for wet outdoor work.

## Key features
- No power cord needed, so it works in more places
- COB LED for safer, cooler operation
- Integrated power bank for charging other devices
- 3 light levels at 1000 lm, 500 lm, and 125 lm
- Rotating head to aim the light where you need it
- Two hanging hooks and a strong magnetic base for hands free use
- Recharges with the included USB-C cable

## Common questions
**How long does the battery last on the NT-5571?**
It runs 1.5 hrs at the 1000 lm high level, 3 hrs at the 500 lm medium level, and 10 hrs at the 125 lm low level.

**How bright is the NT-5571?**
It has three levels, 1000 lm high, 500 lm medium, and 125 lm low.

**Is the NT-5571 water resistant?**
Yes. It is rated IP65 against dust and water jets. The manual still recommends indoor use and keeping the light dry.

**How do I charge the NT-5571 and how long does it take?**
Charge it with the included USB-C cable. A full charge takes about 5 hrs. The charging indicator light turns red while charging and green when it is fully charged. It also has a built in power bank, so it can charge other devices over USB-C.

**What comes in the box with the NT-5571?**
The drop light with its built in 3.7V 2000 mAh lithium battery and a USB-C charging cable.

**What is the NT-5571 best used for?**
It is best as a cordless replacement for a traditional drop light, hung from its hooks or stuck to metal with its magnet for hands free work. It is not made for wet or outdoor conditions.

**How far does the NT-5571 beam reach?**
The beam reaches 99 ft on high, 66 ft on medium, and 33 ft on low.

## Troubleshooting
**The light will not charge.**
This light can fail to charge if it is connected to an adapter that is too powerful. Do not use an adapter rated over 3 amps or 8 volts, because that can trip the battery's overcharge protection and prevent it from charging. Use a standard 5V USB charger with the supplied cable.

## Warranty
1 year limited warranty.

## Safety notes
- For indoor use only, and keep the light dry.
- LEDs are extremely bright. Do not shine the light directly into eyes, as it may cause injury.
- Follow the lithium battery safety guidance in the manual. Do not drop, open, short circuit, puncture, or incinerate the battery, and do not expose it to extreme temperatures.
