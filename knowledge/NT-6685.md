# NextLED Rechargeable Obstacle Sensor Magnetic Pocket Work Light (NT-6685)

## What it is
The NextLED NT-6685 is a compact, rechargeable pocket work light with a built in obstacle sensor that turns the light off when it detects something close in front of it. It has a multi level flood plus a focused spot, a pocket clip, and a strong magnet for hands free use. It is a small carry along task light, so it is not meant to light a large area like a tripod work light.

## Key features
- Obstacle sensor that turns the light off when it senses an obstruction in front of it
- 4 power light modes, a 1200 lm, 600 lm, and 80 lm flood plus a 200 lm spot
- USB-C rechargeable with the charging cable included
- Pocket clip with a powerful magnet for hands free use
- Narrow focused spot beam on top to cut through the dark
- Heavy duty aluminum construction with a strong magnetic base

## Common questions
**How long does the battery last on the NT-6685?**
The flood runs 1.5 hrs at 1200 lm, 3 hrs at 600 lm, and 12 hrs at 80 lm on a full charge.

**How bright is the NT-6685?**
The flood has three levels at 1200 lm, 600 lm, and 80 lm, and the spot adds 200 lm.

**Is the NT-6685 water resistant?**
Yes. It is rated PX7 waterproof.

**How do I charge the NT-6685 and how long does it take?**
Charge it with the included USB-C cable at a 5V 1A output. A full charge takes about 3 hrs.

**What comes in the box with the NT-6685?**
The pocket work light with its built in battery and a USB-C charging cable.

**What is the NT-6685 best used for?**
It is best as a pocket carry light for close up work, clipped to a pocket or held on metal by its magnet, with the obstacle sensor turning it off when it gets too close to a surface. It is not the right tool for lighting a whole work area.

**Why does the NT-6685 turn off by itself?**
That is the obstacle sensor working as designed. It turns the light off when it detects an obstruction close in front of it.
