# NextLED Professional Rechargeable Color Matching UV Flood Light (NT-1010UV)

## What it is
The NextLED NT-1010UV is a rechargeable flood light for color matching across larger areas, with four color temperatures and four brightness levels. It is rated CRI 95 plus for accurate color and adds a UV mode for finding leaks and fluids. A magnetic base, a tripod mount, and a 180 degree rotating head set the light where you need it, and it has an on board power bank. It is an area color match flood, not a pocket detailing light.

## Key features
- 4 brightness levels at 2000, 1500, 1000, and 500 lm
- 4 color temperatures at 2500 K, 3500 K, 4500 K, and 6500 K
- CRI 95 plus rated for color accuracy
- UV light for detecting leaks and fluids
- Strong magnetic base, a tripod mount, and a 180 degree rotating head
- On board power bank with the USB-C cable included

## Common questions
**How long does the battery last on the NT-1010UV?**
Flood runtime is 2 to 8 hrs depending on the brightness level, with the shorter time at the highest level.

**How bright is the NT-1010UV?**
It has four levels at 2000 lm, 1500 lm, 1000 lm, and 500 lm.

**What color temperatures and CRI does the NT-1010UV have?**
It is rated CRI 95 plus and has four color temperatures at 2500 K, 3500 K, 4500 K, and 6500 K, plus a UV mode at 395 to 400 nm.

**Is the NT-1010UV water resistant?**
Yes. It is rated IP65 against dust and water jets.

**How do I charge the NT-1010UV and how long does it take?**
Charge it with the included USB-C cable. A full charge takes about 4 hrs. The wall charger is not included. It also has an on board power bank, so it can charge other devices.

**What comes in the box with the NT-1010UV?**
The flood light with its built in 3.7V 4400 mAh battery and a USB-C cable. The wall charger is not included.

**What is the NT-1010UV best used for?**
It is best for color matching over larger areas, set on its magnet or a tripod, with four color temperatures and a UV mode for finding leaks. It is not a pocket detailing light.

## Warranty
1 year limited warranty.

## Safety notes
- LEDs are extremely bright. Do not shine the light directly into eyes, as it may cause injury.
- This light has a UV mode. Avoid looking into the UV light and avoid skin exposure.
