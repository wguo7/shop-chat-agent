# NextLED Spot Flood and UV Detailing Pen Light (NT-6615K)

## What it is
The NextLED NT-6615K is a rechargeable 3 in 1 detailing pen light with a spot, a flood, and a UV light, rated CRI 95 plus at 6000 K so colors read true for detailing and color matching. It is pocket sized with a clip and two magnets for hands free use. It is a precision detailing pen, brighter and color accurate, where the general mechanic pen light is not color rated.

## Key features
- CRI 95 plus lighting at 6000 K for precision color matching and detailing
- 3 in 1 modes, a spot, a flood with high and low, and a UV light
- Handy pocket clip for hands free lighting
- 2 integrated magnets to mount to metal surfaces
- Durable aluminum housing that is strong and lightweight
- Rechargeable with the included USB-C cable

## Common questions
**How long does the battery last on the NT-6615K?**
The spot runs 2.5 hrs, and the flood runs 1.5 hrs on high and 8 hrs on low, on a full charge.

**How bright is the NT-6615K?**
The spot is 280 lm and the flood is 430 lm on high and 100 lm on low. It also has a UV light at 365 nm.

**What is the CRI and color temperature of the NT-6615K?**
It is rated CRI 95 plus at a 6000 K color temperature for accurate color matching.

**Is the NT-6615K water or dust resistant?**
No water or dust rating is published for it. It is rated IK07 for impact resistance.

**How do I charge the NT-6615K and how long does it take?**
Charge it with the included USB-C cable. A full charge takes about 2 hrs. The indicator is red while charging and green when fully charged.

**What comes in the box with the NT-6615K?**
The detailing pen light with its included lithium-ion battery and a USB-C charging cable.

**What is the NT-6615K best used for?**
It is best for detailing and paint color matching in a pocket size, with a spot for tight areas, a color accurate flood, and a UV mode to find stains, leaks, and ceramic coating errors.

## How this differs from similar NextLED lights
The nearest sibling is the NT-6615 pen light. The NT-6615K is the detailing model, rated CRI 95 plus at 6000 K, brighter with a 280 lm spot and a 430 lm high flood, and built for color matching. The NT-6615 has no CRI rating and states no color temperature, and it runs a 200 lm spot with a 300 lm and 100 lm flood. The NT-6615K sits in the Auto Paint and Detail Lights line, while the NT-6615 sits in the Mechanic Work Lights line.

## Warranty
1 year limited warranty.

## Safety notes
- LEDs are extremely bright. Do not shine the light directly into eyes, as it may cause injury.
- This light has a UV mode. Avoid looking into the UV light and avoid skin exposure.
- For indoor use.
