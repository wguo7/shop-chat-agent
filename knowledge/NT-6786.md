# NextLED Pocket LED Task Light with Spot Light (NT-6786)

## What it is
The NextLED NT-6786 is a pocket sized rechargeable task light that pairs a 500 lm COB task light with a focused spot. It has a magnetic bottom, a built in hanging hook and clip, and an integrated power bank to charge other devices. It is a compact carry along light, not an area light for a whole bay.

## Key features
- COB task light plus a focused spot light
- Rechargeable with an integrated power bank function
- Magnetic bottom design
- Built in hanging hook and clip design
- Heavy duty aluminum housing

## Specifications

## Common questions
**How long does the battery last on the NT-6786?**
Runtime ranges from 3 to 10 hrs depending on the mode. The LED runs about 7 hrs and the COB task light runs about 3 hrs.

**How bright is the NT-6786?**
The COB task light is 500 lm, and the spot has two levels at 300 lm and 100 lm.

**Is the NT-6786 water resistant?**
Yes. It is rated IPX4, which protects it against splashing water.

**How do I charge the NT-6786 and how long does it take?**
Charge it over a USB port at a 5V 1A input. A full charge takes about 3 hrs. It also has an integrated power bank, so it can charge other devices.

**What comes in the box with the NT-6786?**
The pocket task light with its built in 3.7V 1500 mAh battery. It charges over a USB port.

**What is the NT-6786 best used for?**
It is best as a pocket task light with a spot for close up work, hung from its hook, clipped on, or held on metal by its magnet, with a power bank for charging your phone. It is not an area light for a whole bay.

**How far does the NT-6786 spot reach?**
The beam distance is 100 m.

## Warranty
1 year limited warranty.
