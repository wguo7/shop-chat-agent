# NextLED Professional Rechargeable Color Match Inspection Work Light (NT-7885M)

## What it is
The NextLED NT-7885M is the flagship ULTIMATE color match inspection light for paint and detailing work. It pairs a dual color COB flood with a bright SMD spot and a UV light, all rated CRI 95 plus so colors read true. It is built to find paint defects, swirl marks, and sanding marks, and to color match before and after repairs. It is a professional inspection tool, not a general area work light.

## Key features
- CRI 95 plus rated for accurate color
- 4 lighting modes, a dual 6500 K and 3000 K COB flood, an SMD spot, and a 395 nm UV light
- Maximum brightness up to 1500 lm in Turbo
- 180 degree rotating light head to position the beam
- Magnetic base with a hang hook for hands free use
- Magnetic light bar tip for retrieving small dropped metal objects
- One handed lighting control, rechargeable over USB-C

## Common questions
**How long does the battery last on the NT-7885M?**
The flood runs 12 hrs on low and 3.5 hrs on high, the spot runs 26 hrs on low and 19 hrs on high, and the UV runs 48 hrs. Turbo mode lasts 30 sec at a time.

**How bright is the NT-7885M?**
The flood is 300 lm low, 1000 lm high, and 1500 lm in Turbo. The spot is 40 lm low, 80 lm high, and 120 lm in Turbo. There is also a 395 nm UV light.

**What color temperatures and CRI does the NT-7885M have?**
It is rated CRI 95 plus and has a dual color COB flood at 6500 K cool and 3000 K warm.

**Is the NT-7885M water and impact resistant?**
Yes. It is rated IP66 for water resistance and IK07 for impact resistance.

**How do I charge the NT-7885M and how long does it take?**
Charge it with the included USB-C cable. A full charge takes about 2 hrs. While charging the indicator flashes red, and it turns steady green when fully charged.

**What comes in the box with the NT-7885M?**
The color match inspection light with its built in 3.7V 4000 mAh battery and a USB-C charging cable.

**What is the NT-7885M best used for?**
It is best for professional paint color matching and inspection, finding paint defects, swirl marks, and sanding marks, with a UV mode for leaks and a magnetic tip for picking up small dropped metal parts. It is not a general area work light.

**Why does Turbo mode only last a short time?**
Turbo mode runs for 30 sec. After you use Turbo once, you must wait 3 min before using it again.

## How this differs from similar NextLED lights
The nearest sibling is the NT-6692M, the other ULTIMATE color matching light. The NT-7885M is the brighter inspection tool, rated CRI 95 plus with two color temperatures at 6500 K and 3000 K, a flood up to 1500 lm in Turbo, an SMD spot, a UV light, and a magnetic pick up tip, with IP66 and IK07 ratings. The NT-6692M is rated CRI 96 plus and offers five color temperatures at 2700 K, 3500 K, 4500 K, 5500 K, and 6500 K with a memory function, but at a lower 500 lm flood and 200 lm spot and an IPX6 rating. Choose the NT-7885M for maximum brightness and inspection features, and the NT-6692M when you want more color temperature steps for fine matching. Both are ULTIMATE and carry a 3 year warranty.
