# NextLED Rechargeable Mountable Paint and Powder Gun Color Matching Light (NT-R3202)

## What it is
The NextLED NT-R3202 is a color matching light that mounts on a paint or powder gun so you can see true color while you spray. It has three color temperatures and is rated CRI 95 for accurate color, and it detaches from the mount for use as a handheld light. An adapter system fits most sprayer and coating gun barrels. It is a gun mounted detailing light, not a general work light.

## Key features
- CRI 95 rating for accurate detail and color matching
- 3 light modes at 3000 K, 4400 K, and 6300 K
- Secure mounting with an adapter system that fits most sprayer and coating guns
- Detachable light that separates from the mount for other uses
- Wide angle illumination, over 3 square feet of light at 6 to 10 inches
