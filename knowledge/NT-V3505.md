# NextLED Professional Hand-Held Coating Application Light with Diffuser (NT-V3505)

## What it is
The NextLED NT-V3505 is a hand held detailing light made for coating and paint correction work, where it helps you see high spots and other imperfections. It uses a warm 4000 K LED rated CRI 95 plus, and its detachable diffuser spreads the light over a larger area for an even wash. A magnetic base and a flip up hang hook keep it hands free. It is a detailing inspection light, not a general area work light.

## Key features
- CRI 95 plus rated for critical detail and coating work
- 4000 K warm LED
- Detachable diffuser that gives 33 percent larger light area
- Two lighting levels, 1000 lm high and 500 lm low
- Strong magnetic base and a flip up hang hook for hands free use
- Easy access controls and charging port

**How bright is the NT-V3505?**
It has two levels, 1000 lm on high and 500 lm on low.

**What color temperature and CRI does the NT-V3505 have?**
It uses a warm 4000 K LED and is rated CRI 95 plus.

**Is the NT-V3505 water resistant?**

**How do I charge the NT-V3505 and how long does it take?**
Charge it through its charging port. A full charge takes 3 to 4 hrs.

**What comes in the box with the NT-V3505?**
The coating application light with its detachable diffuser and built in 3.7V 4400 mAh battery.

**What is the NT-V3505 best used for?**
It is best for coating and paint correction work, using the warm light and diffuser to reveal high spots and imperfections over an even area. It is not a general area work light.
