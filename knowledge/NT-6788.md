# NextLED Multi-Function Rechargeable Keychain Light (NT-6788)

## What it is
The NextLED NT-6788 is a compact rechargeable keychain light with two light sources, a focused CREE LED and a wider COB LED. It has a sturdy aluminum case, a cap that works as a clamp and stand, and an included carabiner clip. It is an everyday carry and keychain light, not a work light for lighting an area.

## Key features
- Two light sources, a CREE LED for bright focused light and a COB LED for ambient light
- Sturdy aluminum case
- Easy operation light switch
- Cap clamp and stand for hands free use
- Includes a carabiner clip
- Rechargeable over USB-C

## Common questions
**How long does the battery last on the NT-6788?**
The CREE LED runs 30 min on high, 5 hrs on low, and 8 hrs on strobe. The COB LED runs 35 min on high and 5 hrs on low.

**How bright is the NT-6788?**
The CREE LED spotlight is 400 lm on high and 50 lm on low, and the COB LED floodlight is 300 lm on high and 50 lm on low.

**Is the NT-6788 water or dust resistant?**
No water or dust rating is published for it. Its operating temperature range is -4 to 104 °F.

**How do I charge the NT-6788 and how long does it take?**
Charge it through the USB-C port. A full charge takes about 2 hrs. The wall charger is not included and is sold separately.

**What comes in the box with the NT-6788?**
The keychain light with its built in 3.7V 250 mAh battery and a carabiner clip.

**What is the NT-6788 best used for?**
It is best as an everyday carry keychain light, with a focused CREE beam for distance and an ambient COB light for close work, propped up using the cap clamp and stand. It is not a work light for lighting an area.

1 year limited warranty.

## Safety notes
- LEDs are extremely bright. Do not shine the light directly into eyes, as it may cause injury.
