# NextLED Rechargeable Zoomable Neck Light (NT-6763)

## What it is
The NextLED NT-6763 is a hands free neck light that rests on your shoulders and points light where you look. It has adjustable brightness and an adjustable zoom focus so you can go from a spot to a wide beam, a flexible band, and a replaceable battery for continuous use. It is a wearable task light, not an area work light. Some factory paperwork uses the code ZF6763 for this light.

## Key features
- Advanced ergonomic design for easy hands free use
- Adjustable brightness from 100 to 500 lm
- Adjustable focus to choose a spot or a wide beam
- Flexible band to position the light
- Replaceable lithium-ion battery for uninterrupted use
- Magnet and a safety light

## Common questions
**How long does the battery last on the NT-6763?**
Runtime is 1 to 8 hrs depending on the brightness setting.

**How bright is the NT-6763?**
Brightness is adjustable from 100 to 500 lm, with 200 lm and 500 lm in dual mode and 100 lm and 300 lm in single mode.

**Is the NT-6763 water resistant?**
Yes. It is rated IPX4 for water resistance and IK07 for impact resistance.

**How do I charge the NT-6763 and how long does it take?**
Charge it over USB at a 5V 1A output. A full charge takes about 3 hrs.

**What comes in the box with the NT-6763?**
The neck light with its replaceable 3.7V 2200 mAh battery and a USB charging cable.

**Can I replace the battery in the NT-6763?**
Yes. The lithium-ion battery is replaceable, so you can swap in a fresh one for uninterrupted use.

**What is the NT-6763 best used for?**
It is best as a hands free wearable light for detail work, with an adjustable zoom focus to set a spot or a wide beam. It is not an area work light.

## How this differs from similar NextLED lights
The nearest sibling is the NT-6633 neck light. The NT-6763 is the zoomable model with an adjustable focus, a replaceable battery, brightness from 100 to 500 lm, and a 215 ft beam. The NT-6633 is the dimmable wrap around model with a separate switch on each light and fully dimmable output from 15 to 300 lm. Choose the NT-6763 for adjustable focus and a swappable battery, and the NT-6633 for independent dual light control with full dimming.

## Warranty
1 year limited warranty.

## Safety notes
- LEDs are extremely bright. Do not shine the light directly into eyes, as it may cause injury.
